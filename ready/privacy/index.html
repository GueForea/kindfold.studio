Privacy Policy

Ready! Workout Timer · Last updated: October 2, 2026

Ready! Workout Timer is made by Kindfold Studio. This page explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

The short version: The app does not collect any data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

Your workouts, exercises, timer settings and preferences are stored only on your device. The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It makes no network connections of its own.

Free training days

The app counts how many free training days you have used. This counter is stored in your iOS Keychain. If you use iCloud Keychain, Apple syncs it end-to-end encrypted between your devices. We cannot read it.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple’s Privacy Policy applies.

Ratings

When the app asks for a rating, iOS shows the prompt. The app does not learn whether or how you rated it.

TestFlight beta

If you test a beta version through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple may share information with us such as crash reports, device model, iOS version, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. Apple’s TestFlight terms apply.

Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
